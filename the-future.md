Hello, this project maybe will get revived soon in some days but we are not sure when and how. We decided to cease the website operation because we have a lot of things to do and we don't have enough time to maintaining both website and API. We decided to focus on the API more and there will be massive changes to the API soon.

So, lack of motivation is our biggest reason not because of lack of funds and ideas but because we just feel burnt out including our solo dev and the rise of issues still occurs and now Anitaku & Vidstream (stream provider) just stopped working and stopped uploading new Anime all together which makes our site cause a lot of issues and even though we fix it with CORS server, the lack of new update just took a toll on our website so much. The risk of hosting one of these is also high that those legal companies start DMCA-ing so many anime streaming websites right now and we also don't want to lose our name and domain name.

At the end of the day, this is the downside of the free and open-source projects and we can actually announce the shutdown right now but we haven't decided yet because there are so many people who have loved this website since the ``amvstr.ml`` just first released to the public. We are open for contribution once we ready to revive this project.

Please consider trying out other website and APIs with [EverythingMoe](https://everythingmoe.com/) or [TheIndex](https://theindex.moe/) if you want to find the alternatives.

Thank you for your support and we hope you enjoy our website and API that we have made for you. Sorry for all the messes.

From, amvstrm's Team.
